# 📝 Complete Your Portfolio Configuration

To customize the website with your personal details, open the following configuration file:
👉 **[src/data/portfolioConfig.json](file:///d:/Portfolio/src/data/portfolioConfig.json)**

Here are the specific fields you can customize:

## 1. Personal Information (`personalInfo`)
*   `bio`: Add a short, professional summary about yourself (e.g. "I am a passionate software engineer specializing in ultra-low-latency utilities and mobile applications...").
*   `email`: Your email address for the contact form and social links.
*   `linkedin`: Your LinkedIn profile URL.
*   `twitter`: Your Twitter/X profile URL.
*   `resumeUrl`: A link to your downloadable CV/Resume.

## 2. Core Skills & Tech Stack (`skills`)
You can add, edit, or remove skills in the list:
*   `languages`: List of programming languages.
*   `frameworks`: Web and mobile application frameworks.
*   `tools`: Developer utilities, API packages, or configurations.
*   `databases`: Relational or non-relational database management systems.

## 3. Upcoming Projects (`upcomingProjects`)
We have created two placeholders. Change the `title` and `description` to showcase any new apps or research you are currently developing (e.g., WiFi Sensing, Voice Bots, etc.).

---

*Once you save the `portfolioConfig.json` file, the Next.js development server will automatically reload and show your updated data.*
